Privacy notice

Last updated 2 November 2026. This notice explains how Owen Motorsport Ltd (“we”, “us”) uses personal information collected through archieowenracing.com. We are the controller of that information. Contact us about anything in this notice at archie@archieowenracing.com.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, company, email address, phone number (optional), the package you’re interested in and your message.
  • Online sponsorship checkout: your name, company, email address, chosen package, championship, race and logo position, and your agreement to the sponsorship terms.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We receive your name, company, email, billing details and what you bought. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Logo uploads: the file you send, your company name, email and any notes.
  • Emails you send to us, and our replies.
  • Technical data: our host keeps basic server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to run and protect the site. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To reply to enquiries and send the partnership deck you ask for: legitimate interests in responding to businesses interested in sponsorship.
  • To take payment, deliver your sponsorship (branding, hospitality, content and reporting) and send related emails: performance of a contract with you.
  • To keep financial records: legal obligation.
  • To keep the website secure and working: legitimate interests.

We don’t sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that help us run the site and the sponsorship, who process it on our behalf:

  • Stripe (payments, receipts and invoices)
  • Netlify (website hosting and forms)
  • Zoho (email)
  • Google Fonts (the site’s fonts are loaded from Google, which receives your IP address)

To place your logo on the car, helmet or suit, we share your logo and company name with Hitech and the relevant championship for approval. Some of these providers are based outside the UK; where they are, they use safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t lead to a sponsorship: up to 2 years.
  • Sponsorship and payment records: 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Logo files: for the length of the sponsorship and up to 1 year after.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, or ask for it to be transferred. Email archie@archieowenracing.com and we’ll respond within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

If you have any further questions, email archie@archieowenracing.com.
