Archie Owen Racing

Sponsorship terms

Last updated 30 October 2026. These terms apply to Supporter and Partner packages bought online at archieowenracing.com. Principal Partner agreements are set out in a separate written proposal.

1. Who you are dealing with

Archie Owen is under 18. Sponsorship agreements are made with, and payments are received by, Owen Motorsport Ltd on Archie’s behalf (“we”, “us”). “You” means the business buying the package.

2. What you get

You receive the branding and benefits listed for your package and championship on the website at the time you pay, for the races in that championship’s season (or 12 months for the full programme). Your receipt and welcome email confirm the package, championship, chosen race and logo position.

3. Logo approval and placement

  • All logos must be approved by Hitech and the relevant championship before they are applied. We will send your artwork for approval within 7 days of receiving it.
  • We cannot accept brands that are unsuitable for a driver under 18 (including alcohol, tobacco, vaping and gambling), or brands that conflict with team or championship sponsors.
  • Your chosen logo position is honoured where the team’s livery rules allow. If a position becomes unavailable, we will offer the nearest equivalent position or a refund for that element.
  • If your logo cannot be approved at all, we will refund your payment in full.

4. Race attendance and hospitality

Your chosen race is a request. Guest access, paddock passes and hospitality depend on team and circuit arrangements, and we will confirm details at least 14 days before the event. Travel and accommodation are not included.

5. Exposure

We will display your branding as agreed and share partner content as described in your package. We cannot guarantee television coverage, camera angles, results or audience numbers.

6. Changes and cancellations

  • You can cancel within 14 days of paying for a full refund, as long as your branding has not yet been produced or applied.
  • If Archie misses races through injury, a change of programme or events outside our control, we will move your benefits to other races in the same season or refund the unused portion, pro rata to the number of races.
  • If a race or championship is cancelled by its organisers, the same applies.

7. Using Archie’s name and images

You may share the content and photography we provide on your own channels to promote the partnership during the agreed period. Any other use of Archie’s name or image needs our written consent first.

8. Payment

Payment is taken in full when you buy, through Stripe. Prices are in pounds sterling.

9. Law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
