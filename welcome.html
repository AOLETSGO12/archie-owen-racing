Next: send us your logo

Upload your logo so we can send it to Hitech and the championship for approval. Vector files (SVG, EPS, AI or PDF) work best; a high-resolution PNG is fine too.

What happens next

  1. Within 7 days: we send your logo to Hitech and the championship for approval.
  2. Once approved, your branding is produced and applied to the car, helmet or suit as agreed.
  3. At least 14 days before your chosen race, we confirm your guest arrangements.
  4. Through the season: partner content and race photography for you to share.
