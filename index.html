Meet Archie

Archie made his car-racing debut in September 2026 and won in only his second Formula 4 weekend, in the FIA-certified Aramco F4 Saudi Arabian Championship with cars run by Hitech.

  1. Age 11

    Karting begins

    Inspired by watching his dad race, Archie starts karting and works his way up the UK ranks.

  2. 2026

    International OK karting

    Rookie season in the top OK category with Prema Racing and KR Motorsport, including the WSK Super Master Series and the Karting World Championship in Kristianstad, Sweden.

  3. Sep 2026

    Single-seater debut: British F4, Silverstone

    First ever car race weekend, stepping straight into the FIA-certified British F4 Championship with Hitech, one of the most successful junior teams in Europe.

  4. Oct 2026

    Race winner in his second F4 weekend, Jeddah

    Wins Race 1 of the opening round of the Aramco F4 Saudi Arabian Championship at the Jeddah Corniche Circuit, 4.2 seconds clear of the field.

  5. Oct 2026

    Second win ends the leader's streak

    From third on the grid, passes Isaac Seah on lap 3, then pressures championship leader Marlon Bayer at the safety-car restart and takes the lead, winning by 0.592s.

  6. Oct 2026

    P4 and a rookie podium on F4 CEZ debut, Hungaroring

    Hitech's sole entry at the F4 CEZ season finale in Hungary. Fourth overall and second in the rookie class, in a new championship against a new field.

  7. Nov 2026

    Bahrain double podium takes the championship lead

    Wins Race 1 of Round 3 at the Bahrain International Circuit by 0.575s and finishes second in Race 2, moving to the top of the Saudi F4 standings with a 40-point lead.

  8. Nov 2026

    Lights-to-flag win extends the lead

    Second in Race 1 of Round 4 at Bahrain, then leads every lap of Race 2 to win by 2.6s. Eight podiums from eight Saudi F4 races, and a 47-point championship lead.
